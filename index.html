Understand your results

What do the text statistics mean?

Total Characters: The number of characters in your text, including letters, spaces, numbers, and punctuation. Emoji and combined Unicode characters may contain more than one underlying code point.

Total Words: The number of word-like groups separated by whitespace. This provides a practical estimate for everyday writing, posts, and documents.

Characters Without Spaces: The character total after whitespace characters are removed. This is useful when a platform specifies a character limit that excludes spaces.

Estimated Reading Time: A reading-time estimate based on 200 words per minute. Actual reading speed varies with vocabulary, formatting, and subject complexity.

Frequently asked questions

Character Counter & Word Analyzer FAQs

How do I count characters in a text?

Paste or type your content into the text area. The total character count and character count without spaces update automatically.

Does the word counter include punctuation?

Punctuation remains part of the character count. Word totals are calculated from groups of text separated by whitespace.

How is reading time calculated?

The tool estimates reading time at 200 words per minute. It rounds up to the next whole minute when your text contains words.

Can I use this tool for social media and SEO content?

Yes. You can check text length for social media captions, SEO meta descriptions, blog content, proposals, essays, and other writing.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

The counting calculations run locally in your browser. The text you enter is not sent to a server by the counter functionality on this page.
